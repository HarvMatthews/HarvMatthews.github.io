Car Parking Sensor (A-Level)
During my A-Levels, I designed and created a car parking sensor using what I had learned during my time at college. I created my circuit block by block and undertook unit testing to ensure each block worked as intended. As the project progressed, I performed integration tests to ensure that when the blocks were combined, they behaved as designed. I then conducted a series of acceptance tests to ensure that my project met the original specifications.
