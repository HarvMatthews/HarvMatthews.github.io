Climbing App Prototype (Team Project)
In my second year of university, for my group project module, my team developed a climbing app that allows users to search and plan climbing routes, create and share wish lists, and maintain a personal logbook. The project involved:
  • Designing and Devloping a custom Web API
  • Learning C# and the Avalonia Framework
  • Making Helper Functions to ensure integration between back and frontend
  • Following Agile Methodologies
  • Bi-Weekly Meetings to ensure the team stayed on Track
As the project grew, I developed better attention to detail by reviewing and testing code, proofreading written work, and improving my collaboration skills. I also honed my ability to estimate the time and effort required for larger projects.
My Role:
From the start I took on the Role as Team Leader Esnuring the teams success by:
  • Organising Bi-Weekly Meetings
  • Reviewing and Testing Code checking for bugs, System or Runtime Errors
  • Proof Reading the teams work before handing in an Assignment
  • I ensured the team were happy with their roles and the progess
Taking the roles as team leader helped me develop my attention to detail and my collabrotive skills as reading other peoples work and code helped me understand how important writting readable code is.
