Statistics Report Generator (Personal Project)
Main Page
Generates PDF Report with statistics from your downloaded Instagram and Snapchat Data, Used this as a project to gain experience with python and data handling. Reinforced my understaning with JSON and Data Handling, Learnt alot about ReportLab and Data Handling as the project continued.
Displays such stats as:
  • Most Used words
  • Messages Sent
  • Top Friends
  • Amount of Likes given shown as a graph
  • How many accounts you've blocked
